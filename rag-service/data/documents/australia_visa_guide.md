# Australia Immigration Guide

## Student Visa (Subclass 500)

### Overview
The Student Visa (Subclass 500) allows international students to study full-time in a registered course at an educational institution in Australia. This visa covers primary, secondary, and tertiary education, as well as English language courses.

### Eligibility Requirements

**Enrollment**:
- Must be enrolled in a registered course (full-time study)
- Institution must be registered on Commonwealth Register of Institutions and Courses for Overseas Students (CRICOS)
- Have Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from institution

**Genuine Student (GS) Requirement**:
- Must genuinely intend to stay in Australia temporarily for study purposes
- Show strong ties to home country
- Provide evidence of previous study, work experience, and circumstances
- Explain how course aligns with career plans

**Financial Capacity**:
- Demonstrate sufficient funds for tuition, living costs, and travel
- Bank statements, loan documents, or scholarship letters
- Parental sponsorship accepted with supporting evidence

**Health and Character**:
- Meet health requirements (medical examination)
- Meet character requirements (police certificates)
- Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) required

**English Language**:
- Meet minimum English language requirements
- Accepted tests: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, CAE
- Minimum scores vary by course and institution

### Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
- Electronic document from CRICOS-registered provider
- Issued after acceptance and payment of fees
- Contains course details, start date, and duration
- CoE reference number needed for visa application
- Must be for full-time study

### Financial Requirements (2024-2025)

**Tuition Fees**:
- First year of tuition must be paid or funds available
- Amount varies by institution and course

**Living Costs**:
- **Student**: AUD $24,505 per year (as of 2024)
- **Partner**: Additional AUD $8,574 per year
- **First child**: Additional AUD $3,720 per year
- **Each additional child**: Additional AUD $2,790 per year

**Travel Costs**:
- Return airfare to Australia

**Accepted Proof of Funds**:
- Bank statements (in your name or parent's name)
- Bank drafts or letters
- Education loans from financial institutions
- Scholarship letters
- Must show funds held for at least 3 months

### Genuine Student (GS) Assessment
Must provide written statement addressing:
- Circumstances in home country
- Reasons for choosing Australia over other countries
- Reasons for choosing specific institution and course
- How course benefits you (career plans, returnability)
- Any other relevant information

**Tips for GS Statement**:
- Be specific and honest
- Explain course choice logically
- Show clear career pathway
- Demonstrate ties to home country
- Address any study gaps or previous visa refusals

### Application Process

**Step 1: Apply and Get Accepted**:
- Research CRICOS courses and institutions
- Apply directly to institution
- Meet course entry requirements
- Pay tuition deposit

**Step 2: Receive CoE**:
- Institution issues CoE after deposit payment
- Note your CoE reference number

**Step 3: Arrange Health Insurance**:
- Purchase Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
- Must cover entire visa period
- Can purchase through institution or directly from provider

**Step 4: Apply for Visa**:
- Create ImmiAccount online
- Complete visa application form
- Upload all required documents
- Pay visa application fee

**Step 5: Biometrics and Health Checks**:
- Provide biometrics if requested
- Complete health examination from approved panel physician (if required)
- Submit police certificates from all countries lived 12+ months in last 10 years

**Step 6: Visa Decision**:
- Wait for decision (processing times vary)
- Check application status through ImmiAccount
- Visa grant notice sent electronically

### Documents Required Checklist
1. Valid passport (6+ months validity)
2. Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
3. Genuine Student (GS) statement
4. Proof of financial capacity
5. Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) confirmation
6. English language test results
7. Academic transcripts and certificates
8. Letter of offer from institution
9. Police certificates (if required)
10. Health examination results (if required)
11. Passport-sized photos
12. Statement of purpose/study plan

### Visa Fees
- **Application Fee**: Approximately AUD $710 (subject to change)
- Additional fees for dependents
- Payment via credit card, bank draft, or other accepted methods

### Processing Times
- Varies by country of application and time of year
- Generally 4-8 weeks for straightforward cases
- Can be longer if additional information required
- Check Department of Home Affairs website for current times

### Work Rights During Studies

**Work Hour Limits** (as of 2024):
- **During Academic Term**: 48 hours per fortnight
- **During Scheduled Breaks**: Unlimited hours
- Includes paid and unpaid work

**Work Conditions**:
- Cannot start working until course begins
- Must maintain full-time enrollment
- Work cannot interfere with studies
- Can work on or off campus

**Types of Work**:
- Any legal employment
- Part-time or casual roles
- Internships as part of course (unlimited hours)
- Volunteer work (counts toward 48 hours if unpaid position normally paid)

### Maintaining Visa Compliance
To maintain your Student visa, you must:
- Maintain enrollment in registered course
- Maintain satisfactory course progress
- Maintain minimum 80% attendance (or as required by institution)
- Keep OSHC valid for entire stay
- Notify institution of address changes within 7 days
- Notify institution if changing courses
- Comply with visa conditions (work hours, study load, etc.)

### Course Changes and Extensions
**Changing Courses**:
- Notify current institution
- Obtain new CoE from new institution
- May need to apply for new visa if course level changes
- Cannot change to lower qualification level in first 6 months

**Extending Stay**:
- Apply for new Student visa before current expires
- Obtain new CoE for additional course
- Demonstrate continued financial capacity
- Meet Genuine Student requirement again

### Post-Study Work - Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)

**Overview**:
Allows international students to live, work, or study in Australia temporarily after completing studies.

**Two Streams**:

**Graduate Work Stream**:
- For specific skilled occupations
- Skills assessment required
- 18 months stay
- Must have qualification on skilled occupation list

**Post-Study Work Stream**:
- Based on qualification level
- No skills assessment needed
- Duration:
  - Bachelor's: 2 years
  - Master's by coursework: 2 years
  - Master's by research: 3 years
  - Doctoral: 4 years
- Regional study may add extra years

**Eligibility**:
- Hold eligible Student visa
- Completed at least 2 years Australian study
- Meet Australian study requirement
- Apply within 6 months of course completion
- Meet health and character requirements
- Adequate health insurance

**Application**:
- Apply online through ImmiAccount
- Provide evidence of qualification
- Police certificate
- Health examination
- Proof of adequate health insurance
- Application fee: approximately AUD $1,895

### Travel on Student Visa
- Student visa allows multiple entries
- Can travel in and out of Australia freely
- Must maintain enrollment and attend classes
- Ensure visa remains valid before returning
- Carry CoE and visa grant notice when traveling

## Skilled Migration Visas

### Overview
Australia's skilled migration program allows skilled workers to live and work in Australia permanently or temporarily. The program is points-tested and targets occupations in demand.

### Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189)

**Overview**:
- Permanent visa for invited skilled workers
- No sponsor required
- Points-tested
- Live and work anywhere in Australia

**Eligibility**:
- Occupation on relevant skilled occupation list
- Positive skills assessment for occupation
- Invitation to apply (through SkillSelect EOI)
- Under 45 years of age
- Meet points test minimum (currently 65 points)
- Competent English (or better)
- Meet health and character requirements

**Application Process**:
1. Check occupation on Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL)
2. Obtain positive skills assessment
3. Submit Expression of Interest (EOI) through SkillSelect
4. Receive invitation to apply
5. Submit visa application within 60 days
6. Provide all supporting documents
7. Wait for decision

### Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190)

**Overview**:
- Permanent visa with state or territory nomination
- Points-tested
- Must live in nominating state for 2 years

**Eligibility**:
- Occupation on MLTSSL or Short-term Skilled Occupation List (STSOL)
- State/territory nomination
- Positive skills assessment
- Meet points test (65 points minimum)
- Nomination adds 5 points to score
- Under 45 years of age
- Competent English or better

**State Nomination**:
- Each state has own requirements and occupation lists
- May require job offer, work experience in state, or specific occupation
- Some states prioritize offshore applicants, others onshore
- Application to state separate from visa application

### Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) Visa (Subclass 491)

**Overview**:
- Provisional visa (5 years)
- Requires nomination by state/territory or sponsorship by eligible family member
- Must live and work in designated regional area
- Pathway to permanent residence (Subclass 191)

**Eligibility**:
- Occupation on relevant occupation list
- Nominated by state/territory or sponsored by eligible relative
- Positive skills assessment
- Meet points test (65 points minimum)
- Nomination/sponsorship adds 15 points
- Under 45 years of age
- Competent English or better

**Pathway to Permanent Residence**:
- After 3 years on Subclass 491
- Must meet income threshold
- Total 5 years residence in regional Australia
- Comply with visa conditions
- Apply for Subclass 191

### Points Test

**Factors and Maximum Points**:

**Age** (maximum 30 points):
- 18-24 years: 25 points
- 25-32 years: 30 points
- 33-39 years: 25 points
- 40-44 years: 15 points

**English Language** (maximum 20 points):
- Competent (IELTS 6 each): 0 points
- Proficient (IELTS 7 each): 10 points
- Superior (IELTS 8 each): 20 points

**Skilled Employment**:
**Overseas** (maximum 15 points):
- 3-4 years: 5 points
- 5-7 years: 10 points
- 8-10 years: 15 points

**Australian** (maximum 20 points):
- 1-2 years: 5 points
- 3-4 years: 10 points
- 5-7 years: 15 points
- 8-10 years: 20 points

**Educational Qualifications** (maximum 20 points):
- Doctorate from Australian or recognized institution: 20 points
- Bachelor's degree or higher: 15 points
- Diploma or trade qualification: 10 points

**Other Factors**:
- Australian study requirement (2+ years): 5 points
- Specialist education qualification (Australia): 10 points
- Accredited community language: 5 points
- Partner skills: 10 points
- Partner with competent English: 5 points
- Professional year in Australia: 5 points
- State/territory nomination (190): 5 points
- State/territory nomination or family sponsorship (491): 15 points

**Minimum Score**:
- At least 65 points required to submit EOI
- Higher scores have better chance of invitation
- Cut-off scores vary by occupation and round

### Skills Assessment

**Purpose**:
- Verifies your qualifications and experience match Australian standards
- Required before submitting EOI
- Occupation-specific assessing authority

**Assessing Authorities** (examples):
- **VETASSESS**: General professional occupations
- **Engineers Australia**: Engineering occupations
- **ACS (Australian Computer Society)**: ICT occupations
- **TRA (Trades Recognition Australia)**: Trade occupations
- **CPA/CA/IPA**: Accounting occupations
- Varies by occupation

**Assessment Process**:
1. Identify assessing authority for your occupation
2. Gather required documents (qualifications, employment references)
3. Apply to assessing authority
4. Pay assessment fee
5. Wait for outcome (timeframes vary)
6. Receive positive or negative assessment

**Validity**:
- Generally valid for 3 years
- Check specific authority requirements
- Must be valid when submitting visa application

### State and Territory Nomination

**Overview**:
- Each state/territory nominates based on local needs
- Own occupation lists and requirements
- Adds 5 or 15 points to score

**Popular State Programs**:
- **New South Wales (NSW)**: Sydney and regional
- **Victoria (VIC)**: Melbourne and regional
- **Queensland (QLD)**: Brisbane and regional
- **South Australia (SA)**: Adelaide
- **Western Australia (WA)**: Perth and regional
- **Tasmania (TAS)**: Hobart and regional
- **Northern Territory (NT)**: Darwin
- **Australian Capital Territory (ACT)**: Canberra

**State Requirements** (vary by state):
- Job offer in state
- Work experience in state
- High points score
- Occupation in demand
- Commitment to live/work in state

### Application Process - General Steps

**Step 1: Skills Assessment**:
- Complete positive skills assessment for occupation

**Step 2: English Language Test**:
- IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, or other approved test
- Achieve minimum competent English
- Higher score = more points

**Step 3: Submit Expression of Interest (EOI)**:
- Create SkillSelect account
- Complete EOI with all details
- System calculates points
- EOI valid for 2 years

**Step 4: Receive Invitation**:
- Wait for invitation round
- Higher points invited first
- Must apply within 60 days of invitation

**Step 5: Lodge Visa Application**:
- Complete application through ImmiAccount
- Upload all documents
- Pay visa application fee
- Provide biometrics

**Step 6: Health and Character Checks**:
- Medical examination from panel physician
- Police certificates from all countries lived 12+ months
- May be requested before or after application

**Step 7: Visa Decision**:
- Wait for case officer assessment
- May request additional information
- Decision communicated via ImmiAccount

### Processing Times
- **Subclass 189**: 7-12 months (varies by occupation)
- **Subclass 190**: 6-12 months
- **Subclass 491**: 6-12 months
- Check Department of Home Affairs for current times
- Complex cases may take longer

### Required Documents (General)

**Identity**:
- Passport (all pages)
- Birth certificate
- National identity card
- Change of name documents

**Skills and Qualifications**:
- Skills assessment letter
- Educational certificates and transcripts
- Professional memberships

**Employment**:
- Employment references (detailed)
- Payslips and tax returns
- Employment contracts

**English Language**:
- Test results (IELTS, PTE, etc.)

**Health and Character**:
- Medical examination results
- Police certificates
- Military service records (if applicable)

**Partner/Family** (if applicable):
- Partner skills evidence
- Marriage certificate
- Birth certificates of children
- Relationship evidence

### Visa Fees (approximate, subject to change)
- **Subclass 189**: AUD $4,640 (primary applicant)
- **Subclass 190**: AUD $4,640 (primary applicant)
- **Subclass 491**: AUD $4,640 (primary applicant)
- Additional charges for partner and dependents
- Second installment for applicants without functional English

### Working in Australia on Skilled Visa
- Full work rights
- No restrictions on employer or occupation
- Can work anywhere in Australia (except regional visas)
- Can study
- Access to Medicare
- Pathway to citizenship after requirements met
