# Canada Immigration Guide

## Study Permit (Student Visa)

### Overview
Canada's Study Permit allows international students to study at designated learning institutions (DLIs). The permit is essential for any course or program that lasts more than six months.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Valid enrollment at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)
- Proof of sufficient funds for tuition, living expenses, and return travel
- No criminal record
- Good health (medical exam may be required)
- Intent to leave Canada after studies completion

### Application Process

#### Step 1: Gather Documents
Required documents include:
- Letter of acceptance from DLI
- Provincial attestation letter (PAL/TAL)
- Proof of identity (valid passport)
- Proof of financial support
- Letter of explanation
- Photos meeting specifications

#### Step 2: Apply Online
- Apply through IRCC portal
- Pay application fees
- Submit biometrics if requested (must be done within 30 days)
- Upload all required documents

#### Step 3: Medical Exam
- Undergo medical examination if required by IRCC
- Must be completed by panel physician
- Required for stays over 6 months or if from high-risk countries

### Proof of Funds
Financial requirements as of September 2025:
- Show funds for tuition costs
- Living costs: $22,895 CAD for single applicant
- Return travel costs
- Accepted proof: bank statements (28-day rule), bank drafts, scholarship letters

### Biometrics and Medical Exams
- **Biometrics Fee**: $85 CAD
- Required at designated collection sites after application submission
- Processing pauses until biometrics submitted
- Medical exam from panel physician list provided by IRCC

### Documents Required Checklist
1. Valid passport
2. Recent passport photos
3. Application form (IMM 1294)
4. Letter of acceptance (LOA) from DLI
5. Provincial attestation letter
6. Proof of funds
7. Language test scores (IELTS, TOEFL, etc.)
8. Police certificate (if required)
9. Biometrics confirmation letter
10. Educational transcripts and certificates

### Work Authorization During Studies
**On-Campus Work**:
- Available to full-time students with valid study permit
- No separate work permit required
- Can work unlimited hours on campus

**Off-Campus Work**:
- Up to 20 hours per week during academic sessions
- Full-time during scheduled breaks
- Must meet eligibility conditions (full-time enrollment at DLI)

### Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

**Eligibility**:
- Completed eligible program at PGWP-eligible DLI
- Full-time study for at least 8 months
- Applied within 180 days of program completion

**Duration**:
- Length matches program duration (up to maximum 3 years)
- 8-month to less than 2-year program: permit matches program length
- 2+ year program: 3-year permit
- One-time issuance only

**Application Requirements**:
- Must have valid study permit when applying
- Transcripts or official letter confirming completion
- Cannot have had previous PGWP

### Maintaining Legal Status
To maintain valid study permit status:
- Study full-time at designated learning institution
- Report address changes to IRCC within 10 days
- Apply to extend permit before expiry if continuing studies
- Ensure passport remains valid
- Maintain health insurance coverage

### Travel Outside Canada
- Valid study permit and temporary resident visa allow re-entry
- Ensure study permit hasn't been abandoned
- Carry letter from school confirming enrollment
- Keep permit valid throughout absence
- Check visa requirements if traveling through other countries

## Work Permit

### Overview
Canadian work permits are categorized as either employer-specific (closed) or open work permits. Each has different requirements and restrictions.

### Employer-Specific vs Open Work Permits

**Employer-Specific (Closed)**:
- Tied to one specific employer
- Specifies job title and location
- Requires Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) or exemption
- Cannot change employers without new permit

**Open Work Permit**:
- Work for any employer (with some exceptions)
- Available for spouses of skilled workers/students
- Post-graduation work permit holders
- No LMIA required

### Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA)

**What is LMIA**:
- Assessment by Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC)
- Proves no Canadian worker available for the position
- Required before employer can hire foreign worker

**LMIA Process**:
1. Employer applies to ESDC
2. Submits job advertisement proof
3. Demonstrates recruitment efforts
4. Receives positive or negative LMIA
5. Uses positive LMIA to support work permit application

### Eligibility Criteria
- Valid job offer from Canadian employer
- Meet job requirements and qualifications
- Intent to leave Canada when permit expires
- Admissibility to Canada (no criminal record)
- Not restricted from working in Canada

### Documents Required
- Valid passport
- Job offer letter
- LMIA (if required) or proof of exemption
- Proof of qualifications (diplomas, certificates)
- Resume/CV
- Work experience letters
- Police certificates (if required)

### Fees and Processing Times

**Fees** (as of 2025):
- Work permit application: $155 CAD
- Biometrics: $85 CAD
- Open work permit holder fee: $100 CAD (if applicable)

**Processing Times**:
- Varies by stream and country of application
- Check IRCC website for current processing times
- Premium processing not available for most work permits

### Work Limitations
**Employer-Specific Permits**:
- Must work for specified employer only
- In specified job position
- At specified location
- Cannot change without new permit approval

**Open Work Permits**:
- Generally no employer restrictions
- Some exceptions apply (vulnerable sectors)

### Extensions and Employer Changes

**Extending Work Permit**:
- Apply before current permit expires (at least 30 days)
- Maintain status while processing (implied status)
- Can continue working under same conditions
- 90-day restoration period if expired

**Changing Employers**:
- New employer-specific work permit required
- Cannot start new job until approved
- Must meet all eligibility requirements
- May need new LMIA

## Permanent Residence (PR)

### Express Entry System

**Overview**:
Express Entry manages three federal economic immigration programs:
- Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW)
- Canadian Experience Class (CEC)
- Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST)

**How It Works**:
1. Create online profile in Express Entry pool
2. Receive Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score
3. Get invited through regular draws (Invitation to Apply - ITA)
4. Submit complete application within 60 days

### Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW)

**Eligibility**:
- Minimum 1 year continuous skilled work experience (NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3)
- Meet language requirements (CLB 7)
- Canadian educational credential or foreign equivalent (ECA)
- Minimum 67 points on FSW selection factors
- Proof of settlement funds

### Canadian Experience Class (CEC)

**Eligibility**:
- At least 1 year Canadian work experience in last 3 years
- Skilled work experience (NOC TEER 0, 1, 2, or 3)
- Language requirements: CLB 7 (NOC 0/1) or CLB 5 (NOC 2/3)
- Plan to live outside Quebec

### Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST)

**Eligibility**:
- At least 2 years full-time work experience in skilled trade
- Meet job requirements for that skilled trade
- Valid job offer or certificate of qualification
- Language requirements: CLB 5 (speaking/listening), CLB 4 (reading/writing)

### Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)

**Scoring Factors** (maximum 1,200 points):

**Core/Human Capital** (max 500 points):
- Age (max 110 points)
- Education level (max 150 points)
- Official language proficiency (max 160 points)
- Canadian work experience (max 80 points)

**Spouse/Partner** (if applicable, max 40 points):
- Education (max 10 points)
- Language (max 20 points)
- Work experience (max 10 points)

**Skills Transferability** (max 100 points):
- Education + language/work experience
- Foreign work experience + language/Canadian work

**Additional Points** (max 600 points):
- Provincial nomination: +600 points
- Valid job offer: +50 or +200 points
- Canadian education: +15 or +30 points
- Sibling in Canada: +15 points
- French proficiency: +25 to +50 points

### Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP)

**Overview**:
- Provinces nominate candidates based on local economic needs
- Provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points
- Two pathways: base PNP or enhanced (through Express Entry)

**Popular Programs**:
- Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP)
- British Columbia PNP (BC PNP)
- Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP)
- Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program (SINP)

### Application Process

**Step 1: Create Express Entry Profile**:
- Complete online profile with personal information
- Upload educational credentials assessment (ECA)
- Submit language test results
- Enter work experience details

**Step 2: Receive CRS Score**:
- System automatically calculates score
- Profile valid for 12 months
- Can update profile to improve score

**Step 3: Invitation to Apply (ITA)**:
- Receive ITA through regular draws
- 60 days to submit complete application
- Pay processing fees

**Step 4: Submit Application (e-APR)**:
- Upload all required documents
- Submit through online portal
- Pay Right of Permanent Residence fee
- Provide biometrics

### Required Documents

**Identity and Civil Status**:
- Valid passport(s) for all family members
- Birth certificates
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)
- Divorce/death certificates (if applicable)

**Language Tests**:
- IELTS General Training or CELPIP (English)
- TEF Canada (French)
- Results valid for 2 years

**Education**:
- Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) from designated organization
- Original diplomas and transcripts

**Work Experience**:
- Reference letters from employers
- Must include job title, duties, hours, salary
- Pay stubs and tax documents
- Employment contracts

**Other Documents**:
- Police certificates from all countries (18+ lived 6+ months)
- Medical exams from panel physician
- Proof of funds (bank statements)
- Provincial nomination certificate (if applicable)

### Processing Timelines
- **Express Entry Standard**: 6 months from submission of e-APR
- Includes document collection, background checks, medical exams
- May take longer if additional documents requested
- Check application status through online account
