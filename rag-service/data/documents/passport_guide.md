# US Passport Application Guide

## Overview
A US passport is an official travel document issued by the United States government that certifies the identity and nationality of its holder for international travel. US citizens are required to have a valid passport to enter or exit the United States and to travel to most foreign countries.

## Types of Passports

### Passport Book
- Traditional passport with pages
- Valid for all international travel by air, land, and sea
- Required for international air travel
- Validity: 10 years for adults (16+), 5 years for children (under 16)
- Cost: $130 for adults, $100 for children

### Passport Card
- Wallet-sized card
- Valid only for land and sea travel to Canada, Mexico, Caribbean, and Bermuda
- Not valid for international air travel
- More affordable alternative for frequent border crossers
- Validity: 10 years for adults, 5 years for children
- Cost: $30 for adults, $15 for children

### Both Book and Card
- Can apply for both simultaneously
- Bundled pricing: $160 for adults, $115 for children
- Recommended for frequent travelers to neighboring countries

## First-Time Adult Applicants (Age 16+)

### Eligibility
- US citizen by birth or naturalization
- Age 16 or older
- Never had a US passport before, or last passport was issued when under age 16

### Required Documents
1. **Proof of US Citizenship** (original or certified copy):
   - Birth certificate issued by city, county, or state
   - Consular Report of Birth Abroad
   - Naturalization certificate
   - Certificate of Citizenship

2. **Proof of Identity** (original):
   - Valid driver's license
   - Government employee ID
   - Military ID
   - Certificate of Naturalization or Citizenship with photo

3. **Photocopy of Identity Document**
   - Front and back of ID

4. **Passport Photo**
   - 2x2 inches
   - Color
   - Taken within last 6 months
   - Plain white or off-white background
   - Neutral expression
   - No glasses (since November 2016)

### Application Process

**Step 1: Complete Form DS-11**
- Available online at travel.state.gov or at acceptance facilities
- Do NOT sign the form until instructed by acceptance agent
- Fill out completely and accurately

**Step 2: Gather Documents**
- Collect all required documents listed above
- Make photocopies as needed
- Ensure documents are original or certified copies

**Step 3: Get Passport Photo**
- Professional photo service (pharmacy, post office)
- Cost: $10-$15
- Some acceptance facilities offer photo services

**Step 4: Calculate Fees**
- Passport book: $130
- Execution fee: $35 (paid separately)
- Optional expedited service: $60
- Optional overnight delivery: $19.53
- Total standard adult passport: $165

**Step 5: Visit Acceptance Facility**
- Make appointment at post office or library
- Walk-ins available at some locations
- Bring all documents and payment
- Sign DS-11 in front of acceptance agent
- Pay fees (typically separate checks - one for passport, one for execution)

**Step 6: Wait for Processing**
- Routine processing: 6-8 weeks
- Expedited processing: 2-3 weeks (additional $60 fee)
- Emergency processing: Available for life-or-death emergencies or urgent travel

## Renewing Adult Passport

### Eligibility for Renewal by Mail
Can renew by mail (Form DS-82) if:
- Passport is undamaged (or minimally damaged)
- Passport was issued within last 15 years
- You were age 16 or older when passport issued
- Passport is in your possession
- Passport is in your current name (or can document name change)

### Renewal Process

**Step 1: Complete Form DS-82**
- Available online at travel.state.gov
- Can complete online or print and fill out
- Sign and date the form

**Step 2: Get New Passport Photo**
- Must be taken within last 6 months
- Same specifications as first-time application

**Step 3: Calculate Fees**
- Passport book: $130
- Optional expedited service: $60
- Optional overnight delivery: $19.53

**Step 4: Include Payment**
- Check or money order payable to "US Department of State"
- Include all fees in single payment
- Write full name and date of birth on check

**Step 5: Mail Application**
- Include: Form DS-82, new photo, most recent passport, payment, name change document (if applicable)
- Routine service: Use regular mail
- Expedited service: Use expedited envelope
- Addresses provided on form

**Step 6: Track Status**
- 7-10 business days from when application received
- Check status online or call National Passport Information Center

## Children's Passports (Under Age 16)

### Special Requirements
- Both parents/guardians must appear with child
- If one parent cannot attend, provide notarized Form DS-3053 (Statement of Consent)
- If one parent has sole custody, provide court document
- Child must appear in person

### Application Process
- Complete Form DS-11 (do not sign until at acceptance facility)
- Provide child's proof of citizenship
- Provide parental identification (both parents)
- Provide proof of parental relationship (birth certificate)
- Get child's passport photo
- Both parents sign DS-11 at acceptance facility

### Validity and Renewal
- Valid for 5 years
- Cannot be renewed by mail
- Must apply in person with both parents each time

## Name Changes

### Changing Name on Passport

If name changed by marriage or court order within 1 year of passport issuance:
- Free name change
- Submit Form DS-5504
- Include original name change document
- Include current passport
- Include new passport photo

If name changed more than 1 year ago:
- Apply for renewal (DS-82) with fee
- Include name change document

## Expedited Services

### Routine Service
- Processing time: 6-8 weeks
- No additional fee
- For non-urgent travel

### Expedited Service
- Processing time: 2-3 weeks
- Additional fee: $60
- Plus optional overnight delivery: $19.53
- For travel within 6 months

### Urgent Travel (Life-or-Death Emergency)
- Proof of emergency required (death certificate, medical document)
- Appointment at passport agency
- Can receive passport same day or within 72 hours
- Call 1-877-487-2778 for appointment

### Urgent Travel (International Travel within 14 Days)
- Appointment at passport agency or center
- Bring proof of travel (flight itinerary)
- Can receive passport same day or within 72 hours
- Call 1-877-487-2778 for appointment

## Passport Acceptance Facilities

### Types of Facilities
- Post offices (by appointment)
- Libraries
- County clerk offices
- Some municipal offices

### Finding a Facility
- Use facility search tool at travel.state.gov
- Many require appointments
- Call ahead to confirm hours and requirements

### Passport Agencies (for urgent travel)
- 26 locations nationwide
- By appointment only for urgent travel
- Require proof of international travel within 14 days

## Additional Pages (No Longer Available)

Note: As of 2016, the State Department no longer adds pages to passports. If you run out of pages, you must apply for a new passport.

## Lost or Stolen Passport

### Report Immediately
- Report online at travel.state.gov
- Or call 1-877-487-2778
- Prevents someone else from using your passport

### Replacing Lost/Stolen Passport

**In the United States:**
- Submit Form DS-64 (Statement Regarding Lost or Stolen Passport)
- Apply for new passport in person with Form DS-11
- Provide proof of citizenship and identity
- Pay applicable fees

**Outside the United States:**
- Contact nearest US Embassy or Consulate
- File police report if available
- Apply for replacement passport
- May receive emergency passport for immediate travel
- Apply for full-validity passport when return to US

## Passport Validity and Entry Requirements

### Validity Rules
- Most countries require passport valid for at least 6 months beyond travel dates
- Some countries require blank pages for entry/exit stamps
- Check specific country requirements before booking travel

### Recommended Practices
- Renew passport 9 months before expiration
- Ensure at least 2-4 blank pages for stamps
- Make copies of passport data page
- Store copy separately from passport

## Passport Cards vs. Books

### When to Use Passport Card
- Frequent travel to Canada or Mexico by land
- Cruise ship travel to Caribbean or Bermuda
- Less expensive alternative
- Fits in wallet
- REAL ID compliant for domestic flights

### When to Use Passport Book
- Any international air travel
- Travel to countries other than Canada, Mexico, Caribbean, Bermuda
- More widely accepted ID
- Contains visa pages

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I travel with an expiring passport?**
A: Yes, as long as it's valid for your entire trip. However, many countries require 6 months validity beyond your travel dates.

**Q: Can I use my passport as ID for domestic flights?**
A: Yes, passport books and cards are both acceptable for TSA screening.

**Q: What if my child has only one living parent?**
A: Provide death certificate of other parent with application.

**Q: Can I get a passport the same day?**
A: Only in emergency situations with appointment at passport agency and proof of urgent travel.

**Q: What if I need a visa too?**
A: Apply for passport first. Most countries require you to have passport before applying for visa.

**Q: Can I renew online?**
A: As of 2024, limited online renewal pilot program is available for eligible applicants. Check travel.state.gov for current status.

## Special Circumstances

### Second Passport
- Available for frequent travelers who need visa processing while traveling
- Must demonstrate need (frequent travel to countries requiring visas)
- Valid for 4 years
- Apply with Form DS-82 and letter explaining need

### Damaged Passport
- Even small damage can make passport invalid
- Must apply for replacement in person (Form DS-11)
- Damaged passport returned to Department of State
