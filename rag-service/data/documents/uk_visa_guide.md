# United Kingdom Immigration Guide

## Student Visa

### Overview
The UK Student Visa (formerly Tier 4) allows international students to study at licensed educational institutions in the United Kingdom. You must have a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor to apply.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Offer from licensed Student sponsor (university or college)
- Meets English language requirements
- Have sufficient funds to support yourself and pay fees
- Genuine student intent to study
- Age 16 or over

### Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)
- Electronic reference issued by licensed institution
- Contains details of your course and personal information
- Issued after institution checks your financial documents
- Valid for 6 months
- Can only be used for one visa application
- Must start course within 6 months of CAS issue date

### Financial Requirements

**Course Fees**:
- Must show you can pay the course fees for 1 year (or full course if less than 1 year)
- Amount specified on your CAS

**Living Costs** (as of 2024-2025):
- **London**: £1,334 per month (up to 9 months)
- **Outside London**: £1,023 per month (up to 9 months)

**28-Day Rule**:
- Funds must be in bank account for 28 consecutive days
- Final date of 28-day period must be within 31 days of visa application
- Bank statements must clearly show your name, account number, and date

**Accepted Proof of Funds**:
- Personal bank statements
- Building society statements
- Official financial sponsorship letter
- Education loan letter (from regulated institution)

**Exceptions**:
- Not required if studying in the UK 12+ months with valid visa
- If from certain low-risk countries (check current list)

### English Language Requirements
- Must prove English knowledge at minimum B1 level (CEFR)
- Accepted tests: IELTS UKVI, PTE Academic UKVI, others approved by UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI)
- May be exempt if degree taught in English or from majority English-speaking country

### Documents Required
1. Valid passport or travel document
2. CAS reference number
3. Proof of financial funds (bank statements)
4. Tuberculosis (TB) test results (if from listed country)
5. Academic Technology Approval Scheme (ATAS) certificate (for certain subjects)
6. Consent letter from parents (if under 18)
7. Previous academic qualifications and English language certificates

### Application Process

**Step 1: Obtain CAS**:
- Apply and receive offer from licensed institution
- Pay deposit and submit financial documents to institution
- Receive CAS reference number

**Step 2: Prepare Documents**:
- Gather all required documents
- Complete TB test if required
- Obtain translated documents if not in English/Welsh

**Step 3: Apply Online**:
- Complete online application form
- Pay visa fee and Immigration Health Surcharge
- Book biometric appointment
- Upload documents

**Step 4: Biometric Appointment**:
- Attend appointment at visa application centre
- Provide fingerprints and photograph
- Submit supporting documents

**Step 5: Decision**:
- Standard processing: 3 weeks (outside UK) or 8 weeks (inside UK)
- Priority service available for extra fee

### Fees and Costs

**Visa Application Fee**:
- £490 (for courses up to 6 months)
- £490 (for courses longer than 6 months - standard)

**Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)**:
- £776 per year for students
- Must pay upfront for entire visa period
- Gives access to NHS services

**Biometrics**:
- Included in visa fee

**Premium Services** (optional):
- Priority service: faster decision
- Super priority: next working day decision
- Additional fees apply

### Work Rights During Studies

**During Term Time**:
- Up to 20 hours per week if studying degree-level or above at higher education institution
- 10 hours per week if studying below degree level

**During Vacations**:
- Full-time work allowed during official vacation periods
- Check with institution for vacation dates

**On-Campus**:
- Can work on campus anytime without restrictions if employed by university

**Work Restrictions**:
- Cannot be self-employed
- Cannot work as professional sportsperson
- Cannot work full-time unless on placement/internship as part of course

### Graduate Route (Post-Study Work)

**Overview**:
- Allows international students to stay and work in UK after graduation
- No sponsor required
- Can work in any job at any skill level

**Eligibility**:
- Successfully completed eligible UK degree
- Must have valid Student visa when applying
- Applied from within UK

**Duration**:
- 2 years for Bachelor's and Master's graduates
- 3 years for PhD graduates
- Cannot be extended

**Work Rights**:
- Work in any job at any level
- Self-employment allowed
- Can switch to Skilled Worker visa if find eligible job

### Maintaining Status
To maintain your Student visa status:
- Study at the institution named on your CAS
- Attend classes regularly (minimum 80% attendance usually required)
- Report to institution within 10 days of arrival
- Inform institution of any address changes
- Make satisfactory academic progress
- Do not exceed work hour limits
- Keep passport and BRP card valid

### Travel Rules
- Can travel in and out of UK with valid visa
- Ensure your visa hasn't expired before returning
- Carry supporting documents when re-entering
- Multiple entry visa typically granted
- Check visa conditions before travel

## Skilled Worker Visa

### Overview
The Skilled Worker visa (formerly Tier 2 General) allows you to come to or stay in the UK to do an eligible job with an approved employer. This is the main work visa route for non-UK residents.

### Eligibility Requirements
1. Job offer from licensed UK sponsor
2. Job at appropriate skill level (RQF level 3 or above)
3. Salary meets minimum threshold
4. English language requirements
5. Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) from employer

### Job and Skill Level Requirements

**Skill Level**:
- Job must be at RQF (Regulated Qualifications Framework) level 3 or above
- Equivalent to A-level qualifications
- Covers most professional, managerial, and skilled trades roles
- Must be on list of eligible occupations

**Eligible Occupations**:
- Check SOC 2020 occupation codes
- Most professional and technical roles qualify
- Some shortage occupation roles have lower salary thresholds

### Employer Sponsorship
- Employer must have valid sponsor licence
- Licensed sponsor list available on gov.uk
- Employer assigns Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)
- Employer pays CoS fee: £239 for defined CoS, £25 for undefined

### Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)
- Electronic reference number (not a physical document)
- Contains details about job and sponsor
- Issued by employer after they assign sponsorship
- Valid for 3 months from date of assignment
- Must apply for visa using CoS reference

### Salary Thresholds (as of 2024-2025)

**General Threshold**:
- £38,700 per year OR
- The going rate for your occupation (whichever is higher)

**Tradeable Points System**:
- Can "trade" some characteristics for lower salary
- PhD in relevant subject: can go 10% below threshold
- PhD in STEM subject: can go 20% below threshold
- Shortage occupation: lower threshold applies
- New entrants: lower threshold (under 26, postdocs, etc.)

**Going Rate**:
- Each occupation has specific going rate
- Must meet this rate even if above general threshold
- Check Appendix Skilled Occupations for rates

**New Entrant Rates** (if eligible):
- £30,960 per year OR
- 70% of going rate (whichever is higher)

### English Language Requirements
- Must prove English at B1 level (CEFR) minimum
- Acceptable proof:
  - Degree taught in English
  - Pass approved English test (IELTS UKVI, etc.)
  - National of majority English-speaking country

### Application Process

**Step 1: Get Job Offer**:
- Secure job with licensed sponsor
- Employer conducts recruitment process
- Employer decides to sponsor

**Step 2: Employer Assigns CoS**:
- Employer submits CoS request to Home Office
- Pays CoS fee
- You receive CoS reference number

**Step 3: Apply for Visa**:
- Complete online application
- Pay visa fee and IHS
- Book biometric appointment
- Submit documents

**Step 4: Biometrics and Decision**:
- Attend appointment
- Standard processing: 3 weeks (outside UK), 8 weeks (inside UK)
- Premium services available

### Visa Fees and Immigration Health Surcharge

**Application Fees** (depending on circumstances):
- £719 (up to 3 years, from outside UK)
- £827 (up to 3 years, from inside UK - extending/switching)
- £1,500 (more than 3 years, from outside UK)
- £1,639 (more than 3 years, from inside UK)

**Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)**:
- £1,035 per year for adults
- £776 per year for children under 18
- Must pay for entire visa period upfront
- Provides access to NHS

**Other Costs**:
- English language test: varies
- Document translation: varies
- Priority processing (optional): additional fee

### Documents Required
1. Valid passport
2. Certificate of Sponsorship reference number
3. Proof of English language ability
4. Proof of financial funds (if required)
5. Criminal record certificate (if required)
6. Tuberculosis test (if from listed country)
7. Academic qualifications (if needed for CoS)

### Work Rights and Restrictions
- Work for your sponsor in the job described in CoS
- Can do additional work in same occupation code
- Can do up to 20 hours per week supplementary employment
- Can do voluntary work
- Cannot access public funds
- Cannot work as professional sportsperson

### Extensions and Changing Jobs

**Extending Stay**:
- Can extend visa without limit (as long as meet requirements)
- Apply before current visa expires
- Must still meet salary and role requirements
- Same sponsor or new sponsor

**Changing Sponsors/Jobs**:
- Must apply for new Skilled Worker visa
- New employer must assign new CoS
- Cannot start new job until visa granted
- Pay new visa application fee

**Switching to Different Visa**:
- Can switch to other visa routes from within UK
- Examples: Global Talent, Innovator Founder

### Path to Settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain)

**Eligibility Timeline**:
- After 5 years continuous residence on Skilled Worker visa
- Some may qualify after 3 years if salary ≥ £85,300

**Requirements for ILR**:
- Met salary threshold throughout
- Passed Life in the UK test
- Met English language requirement (B1)
- No extended absences from UK
- No breaches of immigration rules

## High Potential Individual (HPI) Visa

### Overview
The High Potential Individual visa is for recent graduates from top global universities. It allows you to work or look for work in the UK without a job offer or sponsorship.

### Eligibility Requirements
- Graduated from eligible global university (within last 5 years)
- Degree equivalent to UK bachelor's or higher
- Meet English language requirement
- Have enough funds to support yourself

**Stay Duration**:
- 2 years for bachelor's or master's degree
- 3 years for PhD
- Cannot extend
- Can switch to Skilled Worker if find eligible job

### Eligible Universities
- Top universities from approved list (updated annually)
- Must check current list on gov.uk
- Universities chosen based on international rankings
- Includes institutions from US, Europe, Asia, Australia

### Work Rights
- Work for any employer (no sponsorship needed)
- Self-employment allowed
- Work at any skill level
- Switch to Skilled Worker visa if eligible

*Note: Check gov.uk for latest HPI requirements as this is a newer route with evolving criteria.*

## Global Talent Visa

### Overview
The Global Talent visa is for leaders or potential leaders in academia, research, arts, culture, or digital technology. No sponsorship required and provides significant flexibility.

### Eligibility Criteria
Must demonstrate:
- Exceptional talent (established leader), OR
- Exceptional promise (emerging leader)

In one of these fields:
- Academia or research
- Arts and culture
- Digital technology

### Endorsing Bodies
Different bodies assess applications by field:

**Academia/Research**:
- UK Research and Innovation (UKRI)

**Arts and Culture**:
- Arts Council England

**Digital Technology**:
- Tech Nation

**Architecture**:
- Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA)

**Fashion**:
- British Fashion Council

### Application Process

**Stage 1: Endorsement Application**:
- Apply to relevant endorsing body
- Submit evidence of talent/promise
- Include letters of recommendation
- CV and portfolio of work
- Endorsing body decision: usually 8 weeks

**Stage 2: Visa Application** (if endorsed):
- Apply for visa using endorsement reference
- Complete online application
- Pay visa fee
- Provide biometrics

### Evidence Required for Endorsement

**Exceptional Talent**:
- Proof of international recognition
- Major awards or prizes
- Media recognition
- Speaking engagements at major events
- Leadership of important projects

**Exceptional Promise**:
- Early career achievements
- Evidence of innovation
- Letters from established leaders in field
- Publications or exhibitions
- Potential for leadership

**Field-Specific Requirements**:
- Tech Nation: product launches, technical contributions, innovation
- Arts Council: exhibitions, performances, critical acclaim
- UKRI: publications, research grants, citations

### Work Rights
- No sponsor required
- Work for any employer or self-employed
- Switch jobs freely
- Start own business
- No minimum salary requirement
- Can work part-time or full-time

### Fast Track Routes
**For Prize Winners**:
- Certain prestigious prizes qualify for fast-track
- Examples: Nobel Prize, Turing Award, Academy Awards
- No need for standard evidence

**For Previous UK Residents**:
- May qualify without full endorsement if previously in UK on similar visa

### Path to Settlement
- Can apply for settlement (ILR) after 3 or 5 years depending on category
- Exceptional talent: 3 years
- Exceptional promise: 5 years
- Must meet continuous residence requirements

### Fees
- Endorsement fee: £524
- Visa application fee: £716
- Immigration Health Surcharge: £1,035 per year
- Priority services available
